THE DETAILS MATTER

Official data.
Clear expectations.

Every card and odds entry comes from Topps’ 2026 documents. “1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling the player on a given row.

A dash in Topps’ odds sheet is shown as “Not listed for this format.” Missing odds are shown as “Not published.” Neither is a print-run estimate. Serial numbering is not inferred from odds. Parallel details describe a checklist family; Topps does not guarantee every subject in every variation.

Multi-subject cards are grouped by card number within each subset. Checklist sections without a corresponding odds entry remain visible. Topps warns that actual contents and odds may vary.
