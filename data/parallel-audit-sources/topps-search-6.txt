Image

Image

Base Parallels make the chase even more exciting, while short prints and variations add spice to the chase.

  * Golden Mirror Base Image Variation: An alternate photo on the card back with a distinctive gold foil finish.
  * Golden Mirror Legend Variations: New to Series 2, this SSP showcases retired legends with an image and a card number in concert with the current stars from the same franchises. For example, Babe Ruth carries the same card number as Aaron Judge
  * Through the Years Golden Mirror Rookie Cards: A special addition new to Series 2, featuring some of The Hobby’s most famous rookie cards from across 75 years of Topps Baseball. Includes Derek Jeter, Mike Trout, Jackie Robinson, and more.

  * Base Card Player Number Variations: Sequentially numbered from 1 up to the player’s jersey number, with the total print run matching that jersey number.
  * Base Card Rookie 1952 Variation: Pays tribute to the iconic 1952 Topps design that included some of the most important cards in the history of The Hobby. Rookies who appear on this checklist get their only 1952 Variation card of the flagship year. For Series 2, that includes Munetaka Murakami, Chase DeLauter, and Sal Stewart, among others.
