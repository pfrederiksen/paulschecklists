L27: 2026 Topps Midnight UFC brings the intensity of the Octagon to life with a premium, hobby-only release highlighted by Midnight’s signature dark, black Chrome designs. As a key release within the Topps Midnight portfolio, it extends the brand’s after-hours identity into the world of UFC, blending sleek design with the raw energy of the sport.
L28: Featuring top fighters, legends, and some of the sport’s most promising rookies like Jaqueline Cavalcanti, Youssef Zalal, and Quillan Salkilld, it delivers a modern, elevated take on collecting under the lights.
L29: This release features a mix of new inserts and returning favorites, including case hits like Twilight and a defining new Autograph Relic set — Fight Glove Relic Autographs, featuring fighter-worn glove relics limited to 8 and 1—each reimagined with Midnight’s signature style that collectors know and love.
