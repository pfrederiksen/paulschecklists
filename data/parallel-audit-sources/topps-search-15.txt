## 2026 Topps Chrome Baseball – Team Samurai Japan Collection Collector’s Box

A Collector’s Box contains four packs of seven cards each, for a total of 28 cards per box. Collector’s Boxes open the door to the release’s full insert lineup. Sensational Samurai spotlights 10 of the program’s most notable performers and appears in about one of every four packs. Generational Greats pairs five sets of Samurai Japan legends, teammates, and position mates, landing in about one of every 12 packs, and includes a dual autograph parallel of Sadaharu Oh and Shohei Ohtani numbered to a single SuperFractor copy. Neon Nights features 10 additional subjects at about one of every 40 packs, while Samurai Spirit is the release’s ultra-rare insert, limited to five subjects with only 50 copies of each plus a single SuperFractor 1/1. The Collector’s Box itself is presented in an Inception-style box built to hold all four foil-wrapped packs, finished with custom Team Samurai Japan branding and cello wrap.
