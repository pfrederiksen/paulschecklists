## The History of Topps Museum Collection Baseball

Topps Museum Collection Baseball was introduced in 2012, replacing Topps Marquee as Topps’ primary museum-themed premium baseball release. The inaugural product featured a 100-card base set, multiple parallel tiers, and a box configuration centered on guaranteed autograph and relic content. Early releases emphasized premium memorabilia cards, including jumbo and multi-player relics, along with on-card autographs.

In the years following its introduction, Museum Collection established signature autograph programs such as framed autograph cards and expanded memorabilia configurations. While formats have evolved over time, the product has consistently maintained its focus on premium materials, limited print runs, and elevated presentation within the Topps Baseball portfolio.

By pairing art-inspired card designs with on-card autographs and premium relics, the product highlights the role presentation, scarcity, and craftsmanship play in long-term collectability, giving it a distinct identity within the broader Topps Baseball portfolio.

## What to Look For in 2026 Topps Museum Collection Baseball?
