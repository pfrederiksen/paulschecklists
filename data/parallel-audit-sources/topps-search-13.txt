## Base Set

The base set of 2026 Topps Inception Football mixes established veterans with a deep rookie class, all rendered in the brand’s abstract, painted art style.

Two new parallels join the lineup this year: Gold Electricity, capped at 50 copies and finished with a burst of metallic rays, and Hot Pink, numbered to 35 copies.

## Rookie Autographs

Rookie Autographs put ink directly on the base rookie checklist, tying each signature to the same design collectors already recognize. The checklist includes ink from names like Ty Simpson and Caleb Downs, making it a stylish way to collect the next generation of NFL talent.

## Launch

Launch returns in 2026 and is built around a bold typographic design and fiery color-burst backgrounds. Ja’Kobi Lane’s stunning card, seen above, shows off the bold, explosive visuals that define the brand.

## Immersion Autographs

Immersion Autographs debuts in football this year as a carryover from the Inception Baseball line, rendering layered streaks of dripping paint behind each player cutout. It’s glorious, colorful celebration, anchored by a fantastic lineup of ink.

## Charged Particles Autographs

Charged Particles Autographs is the other signature subset debuting in 2026 Topps Inception Football, also carried over from the Inception Baseball checklist. A glowing ring and orbiting sparks of color surround each subject, giving the design a lab-like, high-energy feel across its parallel tiers.

## Dual Rookie Autographs

Dual Rookie Autographs pair two top rookies from the same team on a single card. Carson Beck and Jeremiyah Love team up here as members of the Arizona Cardinals’ 2026 rookie class on this Gold parallel numbered to 50 copies.

## Rookie Jumbo Relic Autographs

Rookie Jumbo Relic Autographs contains a large circular piece of player-worn memorabilia alongside a signature. “Jumbo” is the name of the game, as the patches dominate the cards, while the familiar Inception color burst adds an equally stunning backdrop.

## Inception Silver Signings

Inception Silver Signings is a brand favorite, built around striking metallic ink colors, with rare one-of-one versions carrying an added inscription. The negative space on this offering from Dallas’ Caleb Downs is a natural complement to the fiery color of Inception, creating a mesmerizing look.
