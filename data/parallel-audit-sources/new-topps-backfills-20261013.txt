## How to Collect 2026 Topps Chrome® UFC

Date: 13 May 2026

Length: 808 Words

Reading Time: ~5 Minutes

2026 Topps Chrome® UFC delivers one of the most visually dynamic releases in The Hobby, bringing the sport’s biggest fighters into a premium chromium format built around intensity, motion, and finish. From a bold lineup of inserts to a deep parallel structure and autograph-driven chase, the product captures UFC at its most impactful.

2026 Topps Chrome UFC is a premium chromium release featuring a 200-card base set, vibrant Refractor parallels, and a mix of inserts and autograph subsets. With case hits like Let’s Go and chase autos like Octagon Legends Autographs, it offers strong appeal for collectors targeting stars, rookies, and UFC legends.

Topps Chrome UFC has become a defining brand within The Hobby, known for its high-end presentation, vibrant Refractor shine, and ability to translate the energy of the Octagon onto cardboard. Each year builds on that foundation, evolving the design while staying true to what collectors expect from Chrome. With a balance of current stars, rising contenders, and legendary names, the set reflects the full spectrum of the sport.

Let’s take a closer look at 2026 Topps Chrome® UFC.

## 2026 Topps Chrome® UFC の集め方

2026 Topps Chrome® UFC は、The Hobby の中でも特に視覚的インパクトに優れたリリースのひとつだ。UFC 最大級のファイターたちを、激しさ、動き、そして存在感を重視したプレミアム・クローム仕様で表現している。大胆なインサートラインナップ、奥行きあるパラレル構成、そして直筆サイン中心のチェイスを通じて、このプロダクトは UFC の持つ迫力を余すことなくカードへ落とし込んでいる。

2026 Topps Chrome UFC は、200枚構成のベースセット、鮮やかな Refractor パラレル、多彩なインサート、そしてオートグラフサブセットを収録したプレミアム・クロミウムリリースだ。Let’s Go のようなケースヒットや、Octagon Legends Autographs といった注目のサインカードを備え、スター選手、ルーキー、そして UFC レジェンドを狙うコレクターたちに強い魅力を提供している。

Topps Chrome UFC は、The Hobby を代表するブランドのひとつへと成長した。高級感あるプレゼンテーション、鮮烈な Refractor の輝き、そしてオクタゴンのエネルギーをカードへ変換する表現力によって、多くのコレクターを惹きつけ続けている。毎年、その基盤を受け継ぎながらデザインは進化を続ける一方で、Chrome に期待される本質は決して失われない。現役スター、台頭する挑戦者、そして伝説的ファイターたちをバランスよく収録し、このセットは UFC という競技の全体像を映し出している。

## FAQ

  * What cards will be eligible for the UFC buyback offer?
    * Justin Gaethje’s 2026 Topps Chrome UFC base cards and any base parallels will be eligible for the buyback program. For a full list of the eligible cards, please see the table above. Images of the eligible cards will also be provided on the website upon program start.
  * Where can I buy 2026 Topps Chrome UFC cards?
    * You can purchase 2026 Topps Chrome UFC at select retailers like Target and Walmart, your local hobby store, online at Topps.com, or basically anywhere that cards are sold. If you want to visit a Topps Authorized hobby shop, use our store locator!
  * When will the buyback program start?
    * The 2026 Topps Chrome UFC program will begin right after the conclusion of the Main Event on June 14th, 2026, and continue until September 1st, 2026. Shops should not accept Topps Chrome UFC buy back trade-ins prior to the official program start date. This page will update with more details once the winner of the Main Event is announced.
  * Does the buyback offer only apply to 2026 Topps Chrome UFC cards?
    * The Topps Chrome UFC Buyback Offer only applies to 2026 Topps Chrome UFC products.
  * What if either fighter drops out of the Main Event?
    * In the event that either subject drops out of the Main Event prior to the match and the replacement fighter(s) have eligible cards in Topps Chrome UFC, Topps will buy back the cards of the winner of the new Main Event.
  * Will the cards of any other fighters be eligible for the buyback program?
    * No, only the select cards determined by Topps will be eligible for buyback.
  * What will I receive in exchange for my cards? Can I get cash?
    * When you bring your cards into your hobby shop, you will receive store credit in exchange, not cash. The amount of store credit is determined by the pricing table above.
  * How do I determine the buyback value of my numbered cards?
    * All Main Event winner base cards will receive $20 in buyback value. All non-numbered refractors will receive $40. Base parallel cards that are numbered greater than /100 will receive $100 in credit. All base parallel numbered cards less than /100 will fetch $200 in credit.
  * Can I redeem my cards if I don’t live near a participating hobby shop?
    * Yes, you can. Topps is working with authorized mail-in partners who will be accepting mailed-in eligible cards for in-store or online credit. More details will be provided at a later date.
  * I run a hobby shop and want to participate – how can I?
    * If you are a hobby shop in the US or Canada and would like to become a Topps Certified Buyback Shop, please reach out to toppsbuyback@fanatics.com for more information.
  * Is there a limit to the number of cards I can redeem for store credit?
    * While there isn’t an official limit, each hobby shop will have the discretion to cap the number of cards they buy back.
  * Do the cards have to be in mint condition?
    * Cards do not need to be in mint condition. Ripped or altered cards will not be accepted for the buyback program, but as long as your card is in one piece, it will be eligible.
