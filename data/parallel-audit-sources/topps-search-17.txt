## What to Look For in 2025-26 Topps Chrome Black® Basketball?

2025-26 Topps Chrome Black Basketball is built around a curated base set featuring the NBA’s biggest current stars and rookies, presented in Chrome Black’s signature dark, layered design. Collectors can find colorful parallels including the Blue Wave Refractor (numbered to 150) and Green Refractor (numbered to 99).

The insert lineup blends brand mainstays with new additions. Home Court, a popular case-hit insert that originated in the flagship Topps Basketball release, moves over to Chrome Black with a darkened background, while Black Lightning and Nocturnal carry over from their earlier debuts in Topps Chrome Black Football and Topps Chrome Black Baseball, respectively. New to the brand are Carbon Flare and Obscura, both bringing added color and contrast to the release’s darker overall design.

Autographs anchor the product.
