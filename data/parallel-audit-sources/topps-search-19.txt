# Topps Chrome® VeeFriends Product History

Image

## The Story of VeeFriends

Image Debut Year: 2025



Image Licensors: Gary Vee



Image Value Level: Premium



What started as a bold idea in Gary Vee’s sketchbook has grown into a full-blown cultural movement. After making its debut with 2025 Topps Chrome® VeeFriends, the product returns for for 2026, bigger and better than ever.

Topps Chrome® VeeFriends blends creator culture, animated artwork, and premium Chrome technology into one of the most visually distinct releases in The Hobby. Collectors can chase colorful parallels, creator autos, original sketch cards, and bold inserts inspired by the VeeFriends universe and Topps’ signature Chrome finish.

## Who is Topps Chrome® VeeFriends for?

This set is for the core VeeFriends community — those who’ve bought into the brand’s mission of positivity, ambition, and self-awareness. But it also reaches new audiences, including animated art collectors, kids of the digital age, and hobbyists drawn to color, storytelling, and character design.
