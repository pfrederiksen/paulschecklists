## For Set Builders: Collect the Colorful World of Inception

The 2025-26 Topps Inception Basketball base set features 100 cards showcasing standout rookies and current NBA stars from the 2025-26 NBA season. Staying true to the brand’s artistic identity, each card combines bold design elements with premium finishes, creating a colorful foundation for one of basketball’s most visually striking releases.

Collectors can pursue seven different base parallels throughout the set. The lineup begins with Purple (/150), followed by Pink (/99), Red (/75), Holo Gold (/50), Blue (/25), and Camo (/10). At the top of the rainbow sits the ultra-rare Inception parallel, limited to one-of-one, giving collectors a true centerpiece.

## For Autograph Collectors: Discover Dynamic Ink

Autograph collectors have a lot to get excited about in 2025-26 Topps Inception Basketball.

Silver Signings brings together some of basketball’s biggest names with clean, silver-ink autographs. The checklist features legendary icons, current superstars, and top rookies, including LeBron James, Allen Iverson, and Cooper Flagg.

Collectors can also find Gold Ink Inscriptions, featuring personalized autograph inscriptions in gold ink. Numbered 1-of-1, these unique cards combine Inception’s signature artistic design with one-of-a-kind inscriptions from the game’s biggest stars and emerging talent.

Dawn of Greatness Autographs celebrate the highly anticipated 2025-26 NBA rookie class, featuring autos from top newcomers including Cooper Flagg, Ace Bailey, Kon Knueppel, and Dylan Harper. Collectors can also look for the Cedric Coward Blue parallel, sequentially numbered to 25, offering collectors one of the subset’s most limited and eye-catching rookie autograph variations.

Rookie Autographs continue the rookie chase with autographs from the top members of the 2025-26 NBA Draft Class, giving collectors another opportunity to add the game’s next generation of stars to their collections. The set also features a colorful lineup of parallels, including the Red Electricity parallel, sequentially numbered to 16.

Displayed here is Ace Bailey’s Red Electricity autograph.

## Relics: Rookies & More

NBA First Milestone Autograph Relics commemorate one of the most memorable moments in every rookie’s career, pairing an autograph with a piece of the game-used net from the player’s first NBA game.

Each card also highlights the date the milestone occurred, creating a lasting tribute to the beginning of a player’s NBA journey. The above auto relic from budding San Antonio Superstar Dylan Harper’s first game is a particularly exciting option.

Rookie Jumbo Relic Autographs pair rookie autographs with player-worn memorabilia, giving collectors a bold showcase of the 2025-26 NBA Draft Class. Featuring premium swatches from the league’s newest stars, the subset delivers some of the most visually impressive rookie relic cards in 2025-26 Topps Inception Basketball.

Collectors can also collect Blue parallels, sequentially numbered to 25, including the Kon Knueppel card shown here.

> Franchise Fundamental Relic Autographs spotlight some of the NBA’s franchise cornerstones by pairing autographs with premium game-worn memorabilia. The checklist features stars including Victor Wembanyama, James Harden, and more, while collectors can also collect the ultra-rare 1/1 Inception parallel, including this of Paolo Banchero.

Rookie Relic Booklet Autographs pair two of the 2025-26 rookie class’s biggest names on premium booklet cards, combining autographs with player-worn memorabilia.

Featuring pairings such as Cooper Flagg and Dylan Harper, the expansive design delivers relic windows and dual autos in one of Topps Inception Basketball’s premier rookie collectibles.

## 2026 Bowman Football Base Set

The NFL side of the 2026 Bowman Football checklist opens with a 200-card base set split evenly between 100 veterans and 100 rookies. Every card also comes in a Base Chrome NFL parallel version with the same numbering.

The college side runs alongside it as a 200-card Paper Prospects checklist (with a matching Chrome Prospects parallel), covering 88 schools. This is where the majority of the set’s 1st Bowman cards live.

## 2026 Bowman Football Inserts

2026 Bowman Football brings a wide range of engaging inserts, highlighting both the NFL’s top talent and the biggest rising stars from the college game. Collectors can discover a ton of stylish cards in subsets like Gen Next, Bowman Verified, Young Kings, Rockstar Rookies, and more.

## Gen Next

Gen Next is a 25-card insert spotlighting the top quarterbacks and skill players dominating the field during the 2026 college football season. Notre Dame’s CJ Carr is here to be found, alongside names like Bryce Underwood of Michigan and Josh Hoover of Indiana.
