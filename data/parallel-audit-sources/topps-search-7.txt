2025-26 Topps Chrome® Updates Basketball captures the NBA season as it happened. The release brings collectors updated in-game photography, featuring alternate jerseys including Statement, City and Classic Edition uniforms, all while celebrating a historic rookie class and delivering some of the most anticipated Chrome cards of the year.

Headlined by the debut of NBA Debut Patch Autographs, the first Rookie Debut Patch Autographs ever created for NBA players, the release also delivers exciting new SSP inserts, top-of-the-line Chrome autographs, and an eye-catching rainbow of parallels. For collectors of all kinds, it represents one of the most significant and exciting basketball products of the season.

Collectors of 2025-26 Topps Chrome Updates Basketball should look out for NBA Debut Patch Autographs, Chrome rookie cards with updated in-game photography, Alter Egos, Radiating Rookies, Rookie Autographs Lava Lamp, and Chrome autograph parallels featuring Cooper Flagg, Kon Knueppel, Victor Wembanyama, and the NBA’s biggest stars.

Let’s take a closer look at what to watch out for in this essential basketball release.

## Build the Base: Rookies, Parallels & More

2025/26 Topps Chrome Updates Basketball lays a bright and beautiful foundation with a base set built with state-of-the-art chromium technology.

The 200-card base set features rookies, superstars and legends. Veterans feature updated in-season photography and rookies are treated to their first NBA in-game photography cards.

Look out for Hobby superstar Victor Wembanyama’s gorgeous base card in Chrome.

Image

Among the cards that feature alternate jerseys, keep an eye out for this brilliant V.J. Edgecombe Red Refractor limited to five copies, showcasing the rookie in a Sixers Classic Edition uniform.

Collect color-matches in a brilliant rainbow of parallels. This Jalen Brunson parallel card, limited to only 25 copies, is a true standout for fans and collectors of the New York Knicks and the 2026 NBA Finals MVP.

The season’s headline rookies are here as well, from Dylan Harper’s Black parallel limited to only 10 copies, to one-of-one SuperFractors from Cooper Flagg and Kon Knueppel.

Collectors should also stay on the lookout for Denim Tears base card variations, featuring cards from influential designer Tremaine Emory.

## For Autograph Collectors: Find Your Favorite Signature

Chrome Autographs have long been one of the coveted staples of The Hobby, and that tradition continues in 2025-26 Topps Chrome Updates Basketball.

Whether you’re looking for captivating color matches like this Jayson Tatum parallel numbered to 99, or SuperFractor Autos from a historic rookie class, including 1st Team All-Rookie Cedric Coward’s inscribed one-of-one, this elite checklist brings tons of shine.

Popular throwback insert 1980-81 Topps Basketball Autographs brings that retro feel to 2025-26 Topps Chrome Updates Basketball. Collect Anthony Edwards’ signature, as well as a gorgeous Victor Wembanyama card limited to just 2 copies.

Be on the lookout for legends like Hakeem Olajuwon and Allen Iverson, superstars such as LeBron and SGA, and rookies like Dylan Harper, Cooper Flagg, and Kon Knueppel.

A fresh addition to the lineup is Rookie Autographs Lava Lamp, bringing a psychedelic, fluid-color twist in Chrome. This rookie-focused subset is simply mesmerizing in the hand.

## Capture a Moment in Time

For collectors who love to commemorate the historic moments that define the sport, new insert Moment in Time is a phenomenal option. This intriguing design creates the feeling of capturing a moment, and the checklist immortalizes 25 occasions, including Stephen Curry’s famous ‘Ring Me’ celebration.

## 2025-26 Topps Chrome Updates Basketball Collector FAQs

  * What are some of the headline cards in 2025-26 Topps Chrome® Updates Basketball?
    * Top cards include NBA Debut Patch Autographs, Chrome rookie SuperFractors, Alter Egos, Celebración, Captains, Fanatical, Radiating Rookies, Rookie Autographs Lava Lamp, and Chrome autograph parallels featuring the NBA’s biggest stars and rookies.
  * What makes NBA Debut Patch Autographs so important?
    * NBA Debut Patch Autographs feature the game-worn debut patch from a player’s first NBA jersey paired with an autograph on a one-of-one card. Making their basketball debut in this release, they instantly become some of the most significant rookie cards in The Hobby.
  * Which players headline 2025-26 Topps Chrome® Updates Basketball?
    * The checklist includes stars and rookies such as Cooper Flagg, Dylan Harper, Kon Knueppel, Victor Wembanyama, LeBron James, Stephen Curry, Luka Dončić, Shai Gilgeous-Alexander, Kevin Durant, Jayson Tatum, and Jalen Brunson.
  * What new inserts are available in 2025-26 Topps Chrome® Updates Basketball?
    * The release introduces new inserts including Celebración, Captains, Moment in Time, and Rookie Autographs Lava Lamp.
