L58: ## Marvel Diamonds
L59:
L60: Marvel Diamonds is the largest individual checklist in the product at 91 subjects, spanning classic Marvel Comics characters and modern MCU stars alike. The concept starts at Silver, numbered to 50, and climbs through Black, Red, and Gold parallels — each embedded with real gemstones for a premium, tactile finish. Iron Man appears above in a 1/1 Gold version.
L61: A separate 27-card Marvel Diamond Autographs lineup follows the same tiered parallel structure, pairing the Diamonds design with signatures. Captain America stars in a Gold 1/1 version of the autograph cite46†variation .
L62: ## True Greatness
L63:
L64: True Greatness runs alongside Marvel Diamonds as a 32-subject set spanning major characters from throughout the Marvel Cinematic Universe — including Iron Man, Captain America, Thor, Valkyrie, Loki, Black Widow, and more. The set starts at Black, numbered to 10, with a full True Greatness Autographs checklist following the same tiered structure.
L65:
L66: Valkyrie, played by Tessa Thompson, headlines the autograph checklist here in a Red parallel numbered to just 5 copies.
L67: ## Ink 616
L68:
L69: Ink 616 is a single-signer, autograph concept named for Earth-616, the designation for the primary Marvel Comics universe. Thanos anchors the checklist here in a striking Gold 1/1, his signature laid directly across a cosmic, Earth-backdropped design.
L70: ## Laced in Gold
L71:
L72: Laced in Gold is an autograph insert signed in gold pen, framed by a sharp geometric border and topped with a bold gold nameplate. Chris Evans headlines the checklist here as Captain America, signed on a Red parallel numbered to just 5 copies.
L73: ## The Vault
L74:
L75: The namesake insert of the release, The Vault is built as a two-fold book card that opens to reveal an autograph inside, starting at Gold and capping out at 1/1. The card’s closed cover leans directly into the product’s central theme, presenting collectors with an actual vault door design before they “unlock” the signature within.
L76:
L77: A related four-fold book card, Asgard Literature, also appears in the checklist for collectors looking for multi-panel autograph pieces.
L78: ## Vault Armory
L79:
L80: Vault Armory is a 10-subject relic insert built around the signature weapons tied to major Marvel characters, including Thor, Star-Lord, and Captain America. The Captain America version shown above pairs an autograph with a relic swatch tied to his iconic shield in a 1/1 version.
L81: ## From the Set
L82:
L83: An 11-subject relic insert, From the Set pulls its memorabilia and imagery directly from newer MCU titles — Ironheart, Agatha All Along, and Loki — pairing relic swatches with character photography from each show.
L84:
L85: Tom Hiddleston headlines the checklist here as Loki, pairing an autograph with a relic piece numbered to just 10 copies.
L86: ## Duos & Triple Autographs
L87:
L88: Combining key characters on a single card, Duos Autographs and Triple Autographs pair multiple signers together, each numbered to 25. The Triple Autograph above brings together Bucky Barnes, John F. Walker, and Valentina Allegra de Fontaine in a 1/1 version — a trio pulled straight from the world of The Falcon and the Winter Soldier and Thunderbolts.
L89: Autograph Relic versions of these multi-signed cards — Duos Autograph Relics and Triple Autograph Relics — add memorabilia into the mix.
L90: ## Sketch Cards
L91:
L92: Every 2026 Topps Vault Marvel box also includes a Vault Originals box topper — a 6.5″ x 4.25″ original-art card printed on thick 76pt stock, featuring artwork by Ariel Diaz and available in Black, Red, and Gold parallels.
L93:
L94: Rounding out the release, Premiere Sketches and Golden Sketches bring original hand-drawn artist sketches to the checklist, alongside carryover autograph content from the Marvel On-Card line, including Opulence Autographs and Immortal Autographs.
