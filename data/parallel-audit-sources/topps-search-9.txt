Few products on the football calendar are designed to stand out quite like 2025 Topps Cosmic Chrome Football.

Making its debut on the gridiron, Cosmic Chrome takes the familiar Chrome formula and launches it into a universe of bold colors, space-themed designs, and ambitious chase cards. The result is a release that feels different from traditional football products while still delivering the rookie cards, parallels, inserts, and autograph content collectors expect from a premium Chrome release.

The 2025 Topps Cosmic Chrome Football Hobby Box brings Cosmic Chrome to the gridiron for the first time, combining a 200-card checklist, colorful refractor parallels, premium inserts, and autograph content. Collectors can expect numbered parallels, chase-worthy case hits, and a deep rookie, veteran, and legend lineup.

## What’s Inside the Hobby Box?

Each Hobby Box contains 20 packs with four cards per pack, giving collectors 80 cards to explore.

According to Topps, Hobby Boxes deliver:

  * Two numbered parallels per box
  * 10 inserts per box
  * Three base parallels per box, including one Nucleus Refractor and two additional Refractors

That combination creates a steady mix of color, rarity, and insert content throughout a break. Whether you’re ripping packs or sorting cards afterward, there is rarely a stretch where something interesting isn’t hitting the table.

## A Parallel Rainbow Built for Chrome Collectors

Cosmic Chrome leans heavily into what Chrome collectors enjoy most: colorful parallels.

The 200-card base set is supported by a deep parallel lineup that ranges from Aqua Equinox Refractors numbered to 199 down to SuperFractor 1/1s. Along the way, collectors can chase Purple Nebula, Blue Moon, Green Space Dust, Gold Interstellar, Orange Galactic, Black Eclipse, Lunar Eclipse, and Red Flare variations.

The naming convention fits perfectly with the product’s cosmic theme, but these aren’t just visual upgrades. For player collectors and rainbow chasers, the extensive parallel structure provides multiple levels of rarity and long-term collecting goals.

These cards showcase some of the most creative artwork in the product line and fully embrace the cosmic identity that sets this release apart from more traditional football offerings.

Collectors can also chase Starfractor and Planetarium cards, both of which add another layer of rarity and visual appeal to the product’s insert lineup.
