Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

All 764 numbered subject rows in 31 sections reconciled, including both 200-card base and Clawed programs, 30 retail insert rows, the wrapped quad-facsimile heading and both printed inline cards 136 Mary Jane / 137 Misery. All 38 unlabelled additional cells retain their printed artist names or years without assigning new column roles. Artist Originals lists two unnumbered artists; the separate sketch roster retains 124 names, including IQ and the final Japanese/Latin name. No team or rookie fields are inferred. All 179 odds categories and seven literal columns remain unchanged, including three-part and period-separated ratios; those values are not converted to numeric hit rates. The guide describes the Stan Lee/Steve Ditko cut program and Artist Originals as one-of-one; these do not number the separate five-character The One and Only insert. Exhaustive parallel numbering and a release date remain unverified.
