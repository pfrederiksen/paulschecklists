THE DETAILS MATTER

Official data.
Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

Partial calendar-year archive: only entries with a verified official sale date on or after January 1, 2026 are included. Entries without a verified sale date remain pending. Official active products, individual product pages and captured pages of the Product Year 2026 archive supply the evidence. All 95 archive pages have been visited; individual entries and product details still require reconciliation, and some product pages return only a loading shell. Archive-only observations retain the printed number, subject and print run; dates and parallel terms appear only where verified on the product page. Non-numbered bundles and entertainment products need separate reconciliation. Product year and season labels do not establish a calendar-year release date. Blank print runs mean not published in the observed source. Print runs are not pack odds.
