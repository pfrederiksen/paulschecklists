Official sources & import notes

THE DETAILS MATTER

Official data.
Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

All 338 physical entries across eight sections are reconciled, retaining 100 base numbers, separate player/team cells and 154 literal Rookie marks. Original accents, Seattle Supersonics and the single printed Portland Trailblazers spelling remain intact. The final-page CI-YK row is retained. All 60 ratios are paired with their physically aligned captions, including Base 4:1; the larger value glyphs begin three points above the caption. The heading prints only Odds, without a named format or ratio unit. No Hobby format, pack rate or serial limit is inferred from box contents or ratio values. The official June 11 checklist spotlight explicitly announces June 19, 2026 for the main release; article publication and the separate All-Star product remain distinct. Reviewed guides confirm the eight checklist program sizes but do not establish a numeric serial-number ladder. Exhaustive parallel numbering remains unconfirmed.
