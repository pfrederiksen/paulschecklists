THE DETAILS MATTER

Official data.
Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

All 169 printed entries and 52 literal SP/SSP marks from the two-page official PDF are retained. The second currently linked one-page PDF contains 108 matching entries and omits 61; neither link establishes a revision date. Repeated Private Signings codes and separate marked/unmarked versions remain separate source rows. Pinnacle Mascot is an empty printed heading, with no published card roster. The official CSV export is header-only; its evidence is retained separately. No teams, rookie flags, serial limits or pack odds are printed in these checklists. SP/SSP are source designations, not numeric odds or serial numbers. Card-image handwriting is not imported. The March 13, 2025 announcement and preorder wording do not establish a general release date. Exhaustive product-guide/parallel reconciliation and calendar-year release coverage remain incomplete.
