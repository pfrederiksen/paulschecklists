THE DETAILS MATTER

Official data.
Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

All 68 physical card entries across ten sections are reconciled, including alphabetic RSSP in RUBY SUPER SHORT PRINT. Original subject and photoshoot descriptions are preserved; no team or rookie fields are published. All 58 category ratios are explicitly per pack, without a named format. The product Description supplies 51 family-scoped numbering specifications: 50 exact odds joins and one separate Superfractor autograph-relic specification. The odds-only autograph-relic Foilfractor retains its ratio without an inferred /1 limit. Unnumbered categories stay unnumbered and box contents stay separate from odds. Exhaustive parallel coverage remains unconfirmed.
