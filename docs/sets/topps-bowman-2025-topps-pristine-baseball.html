Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

All 814 physical subject rows / 808 family-card identities across 18 sections retain original Name/Team cells, 300 base numbers and 224 literal Rookie marks. All six dual cards retain both signers. Historical teams, trademarks and card-specific assignments remain unchanged. All 89 odds categories retain the literal FGC006155 Hobby header and values, including 01:16:00; no ratio unit is printed. Six product-description 1/1 terms join only Base Pristine Black, the four named short-print Superfractors and All Star Game Pristine Pieces. Other color limits are not inferred. The guide’s 10 cards per tri-pack and store’s 8 cards per pack retain their original units without choosing a corrected count.
