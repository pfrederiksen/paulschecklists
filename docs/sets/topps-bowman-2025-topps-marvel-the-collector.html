THE DETAILS MATTER

Official data.
Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

All 262 numbered subject rows and 34 unnumbered artists across 12 sections are reconciled using the original PDF clipping cells. Physical columns retain Character/Property values for MCU Perfection and Marvel Tomorrow and both unlabelled autograph text columns. Show Stoppers ST-8 retains its full two-line Black Widow Emerges Victorious caption. Wrapped autograph, Asgard, Museum and sketch headings remain single programs; all five duals retain both subjects. All 59 odds categories and literal ratios are preserved. The printed odds column names the product, not a pack format or ratio unit; the format control is disabled and no per-pack rate is inferred. Store Product Year 2026, Available From February 4, 2026 and the guide Debut Year 2026 remain separate from the 2025 checklist title and an unverified calendar release date.
