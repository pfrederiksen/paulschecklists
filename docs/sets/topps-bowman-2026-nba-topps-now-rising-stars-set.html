THE DETAILS MATTER

Official data.
Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

All 37 original PDF rows retain Player, Position, Status and Team, including the second-page Kyshawn George 21-A continuation. Team Melo, Team T-Mac and Team Vince are the published teams; NBA club assignments are not supplied. The original Status column retains 17 literal RC marks and 20 blanks. Five foil limits /50, /25, /10, /5 and /1 stay in BASE CARDS. Rising Stars Parallel has no published numbering status; AUTOGRAPH SUBJECTS retains only the qualified /5-or-lower upper bound. Bundle PR7287 and February 9–16, 2026 availability do not establish per-card print runs, pack odds or a general release date. Exhaustive eligibility and historical NOW coverage remain unverified.
