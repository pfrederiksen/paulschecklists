THE DETAILS MATTER

Official data.
Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

All 593 printed subject rows across 22 sections retain their physical Name, Team and Rookie cells, including all 100 base numbers. Historical team assignments, accents, literal Minnesota Tmberwolves and Karl Anthony-Towns spellings are retained without correction. All 219 odds categories retain their literal unlabelled ratios; the PDF names neither format nor unit, so separate box configuration does not turn them into per-pack odds. The NIGHT SHADE / Nightshade spelling alias is exact and family-specific. The official January 12 guide confirms January 29, 2026 release despite the 2025-26 product label. Neither checked official page publishes a named numbering ladder; serial limits and exhaustive parallel numbering remain unverified.
