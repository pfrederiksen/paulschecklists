Official sources & import notes

THE DETAILS MATTER

Official data.
Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

All 346 printed subject rows, 200 distinct base numbers, separate Team/Subset fields and 35 literal Rookie flags are reconciled across the three nine-page print segments by identical physical row boxes. The 200-card base program has 210 subject rows because printed duo numbers 164–173 each repeat for their two subjects. Blank teams and original spelling remain unchanged. All 274 odds categories and seven original format columns remain in officialOddsTable; this page displays only the 51 rows with published Sapphire Pack Rate values, including repeated autograph rows. The official launch page states the collection has arrived. The Japanese store lists Product Year 2026 and Available From February 13, 2026, while the checklist/product title identifies 2025. Regional store availability is not a verified universal release date. Exhaustive parallel-numbering review remains pending; unrelated Chrome format limits are not applied to Sapphire.
