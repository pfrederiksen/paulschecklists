Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

All 425 numbered subject rows / 395 family-card identities and 86 unnumbered artists across 24 sections are reconciled against original PDF text-object columns. The 100 BASE entries and 35 30 YEARS AND BEYOND entries are separate programs; the numeric-leading heading is a caption. Character/Property and First Name/Last Name/Character cells remain separate, including repeated dual signers, three image-variation 25 entries and Tinny’s printed Toy Story 2 variation property. All 104 Hobby odds and literal values are preserved; no ratio unit is named. The guide supplies 47 exact family limits: 42 join existing odds and five remain specification-only, without invented ratios. Guide-only autograph example spellings, store availability and box contents remain qualified. A general release date and exhaustive parallel coverage remain unverified.
