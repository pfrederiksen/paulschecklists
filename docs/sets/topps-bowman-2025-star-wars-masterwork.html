THE DETAILS MATTER

Official data.
Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

All 660 printed numbered subject rows and 208 unnumbered sketch artists are reconciled to physical PDF cells. Repeated multi-signer card numbers and original unlabelled actor/character/relic text columns are retained separately; counts represent source entries, not unique cards. All 90 printed odds categories and the literal SKU 1 Pack Rate heading are retained. Exhaustive parallel-numbering coverage and a calendar release date have not been established. Skeleton Crew Auto Relic Black is printed as 1,249 without a ratio separator; it remains literal with no inferred probability. The full underlying Lothal Jedi Temple Mortis Gods Painting Puzzle caption is retained even though the printed cell clips it.
