Official sources & import notes

THE DETAILS MATTER

Official data.
Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

All 704 numbered subject rows across 30 sections and 116 unnumbered sketch artists reconciled against physical cells. The three base tiers contain 100 subject rows but 98 distinct numeric identities: printed 34/034 and 41/041 repeat, while numeric 36 and 42 are absent. Preserve leading zeros and the published 19/46 forms. Blacklight, Negative Zone and Tribute each retain all 100 printed codes. Actor/character, artist and comic-issue cells remain separate unlabelled source fields. Repeated dual/triple/quad signer numbers and the missing FS-10 are retained. All 158 odds categories retain their complete wrapped names and literal Hobby ratios from ruled cells. The odds sheet names Hobby but does not state a ratio unit; no per-pack rate or probability is inferred. Only Tribute to Finest 96 /96 and Fantastic Creators 1/1 have guide-confirmed numbering; other parallel limits and a calendar release date remain unverified.
