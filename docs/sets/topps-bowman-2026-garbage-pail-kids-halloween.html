THE DETAILS MATTER

Official data.
Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

All 105 numbered card rows and 53 unnumbered sketch artists across six checklist sections retain the printed Subset, Name and Card Back / autograph-artist cells. The 50 Base, 25 C Names, ten Scream Queens, ten Art Variation and ten autograph rows remain distinct, including matching A/B codes and five blank Scream Queens backs. All 12 literal SINGLE BOX odds are imported from the product-page PDF, not converted to per-pack rates. Five Base Slime limits are joined only to the corresponding Base Foil odds names, preserving both published names and /50, /25, /10, /5 and /1 limits. Wrapper Rainbow Foil has no published limit; the 25-subject wrapper summary is not expanded into an inferred roster. Final Artwork Redemption is odds-only. Release is scheduled October 22; exhaustive parallel reconciliation remains unconfirmed.
