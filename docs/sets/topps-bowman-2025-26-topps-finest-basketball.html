Official data.
Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

All 764 original subject rows across 17 sections are reconciled to physical Name/Team cell origins. All 300 tiered base numbers remain intact. The PDF publishes no Rookie column: the shared rookie control stays disabled, while guide annotations remain separate. All 217 odds categories retain original Hobby/Breaker columns, blank cells, literal Base Common 5:1 and Geometric 2:1; the sheet names no ratio unit. Combining accents, printed Cade Cunnigham, Philadelpia 76ers, Seattle Supersonics and Portland Trailblazers spellings remain unchanged. The official Flagg Black Geometric 1/1 pull lacks an exact printed number/tier join and remains qualified. Historical rookie-card limits never number this release.
