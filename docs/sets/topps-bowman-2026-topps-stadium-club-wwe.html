THE DETAILS MATTER

Official data.
Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

Official product page lists October 23 availability. Physical name/designation columns overlap for long names in PDF text extraction; original glyph draw order preserves every printed character. Raw, Smackdown, NXT, Legend and other designations are preserved as published fields, not inferred teams. Source ratios such as 5:1 mean five cards per pack, not one-in-five pack odds.
