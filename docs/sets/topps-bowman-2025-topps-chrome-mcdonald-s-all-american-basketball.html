Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

All 400 original rows / 12 sections are reconciled to physical Name and East/West cell origins, with all 100 base numbers. East/West designations remain separate from NBA teams; no rookie field is published. Original ZAKiyah / ZaKiyah / Zakiyah and Lebron / LeBron spellings remain unchanged. All 73 Hobby categories retain literal values, including Base 9:1 and Event Autogrpahs. The header does not state a ratio unit. Concrete Canvas has no published odds join. The official guide explicitly confirms March 12, 2026 release, separately from the 2025 title and store Product Year 2026.
