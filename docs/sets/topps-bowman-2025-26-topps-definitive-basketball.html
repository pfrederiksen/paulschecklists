Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

All 1,024 physical subject rows / 979 family-card identities across 27 original families retain separate Name/Team cells and 298 literal Rookie marks. The production disclaimer is source text, not a card. Repeated dual/triple signer codes, accents and original historical team assignments remain intact. All 141 odds categories retain both original HOBBY and FDI columns, including null FDI Gold cells and the FDI-only Rookie Patch First Day Issue row. The source does not name a ratio unit, so no pack rate is inferred. Five exact Gold 1/1 family joins and both 1/1 Nameplate programs retain official product scope. Card-specific guide and pull-report examples remain separate from family limits; unnamed intermediate colors and the Triple Autographs /10 family statement remain qualified.
