Official sources & import notes

THE DETAILS MATTER

Official data.
Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

All 394 printed subject rows / 336 family-card identities across 15 sections retain physical Character, Subject, Property, First Name, Last Name, Character Name, Team, Division and unlabelled DEBUT fields. All 200 base numbers and the 30 MLBH-31 through MLBH-60 team assignments remain as printed; teams are blank outside the jersey program. Repeated dual, triple and quad actor/character rows retain every signer. BASE CARDS labels the unheaded first table; no rookie flags or absent Property values are inferred. All 54 Sapphire odds preserve literal 01:14:00, 1:26854 and Padpradascha spelling. The Sapphire format is published but no ratio unit is named; box contents do not establish per-pack odds. Named serial limits and an exact release date are unverified; no regular Chrome numbering is copied into Sapphire.
