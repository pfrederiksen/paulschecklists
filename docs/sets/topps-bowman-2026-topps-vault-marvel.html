THE DETAILS MATTER

Official data.
Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

All 377 numbered source rows are reconciled to physical Actor, Character, Relic / prop and Artist columns; repeated numbers for multiple signers are preserved. All 105 sketch-artist entries are reconciled to original PDF cell clipping boxes, including eight wrapped names. Counts are source entries, not unique cards or artists. All 74 published odds entries are imported. Exact release date is unverified.
