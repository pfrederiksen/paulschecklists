Official sources & import notes

THE DETAILS MATTER

Official data.
Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

All 239 coded subject rows and 77 unnumbered sketch artists across 16 sections are reconciled against original physical PDF rows. LC Lump of Coal is retained; the 50-card base and both 25-row Characters A/B lists remain separate. The wrapped Jordan "Slippa Chervascus" Gumbrell artist is a single entry. Printed CCA-GTA and CCA-MRO differ from their non-autograph codes; all original titles and spellings are retained. The official product landing page supplies odds absent from the global catalog: all 70 Hobby categories retain 65 literal ratios and five unlisted Present Mini values. No ratio unit is named. Exact guide joins number only Base Cards Candy Cane /299 and Snowcaps /5; extended character allocation, illustrated insert examples and other numbering remain qualified. Box contents and store availability are not pack odds or a verified general release date. Exhaustive parallel reconciliation remains incomplete.
