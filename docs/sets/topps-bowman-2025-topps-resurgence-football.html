THE DETAILS MATTER

Official data.
Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

All 749 physical subject rows across 19 original sections retain separate Name, Team and 379 literal Rookie fields. Base Signatures and Rookie Signatures remain distinct, including their exact veteran/rookie odds joins. The 200 base entries print numbers 1–202 with 106 and 183 absent; the official guide instead describes 201 entries / 101 rookies. Neither source is silently corrected. All 249 odds categories retain four literal Pack Rate columns; the production disclaimer is not a format. Base Prism Mega 0.05 remains an uninterpreted published cell. Crimson Surge /199 joins only the veteran and rookie base categories. Neon Initiates Gold Power Surge is limited to 50 copies, with serial marking unstated. Named SuperFractor and Jalen Hurts /15 examples remain card-specific, without numbering other cards. Exhaustive parallel numbering and a general release-date statement remain unverified; article dates and box contents do not establish either.
