THE DETAILS MATTER

Official data.
Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

All 878 physical subject rows / 863 family-card identities across 18 sections are reconciled, with original Name/Team cells and 373 literal Rookie marks. The 15 Star Clusters numbers each retain both subjects and their individual teams/flags. All 113 Hobby/Lunar odds categories retain literal values, including Base 3:1; the sheet states no ratio unit. Four exact guide joins retain Aqua Equinox /199 and Superfractor 1/1 only for the 200-card base program, split across Base and Rookies. Equinox Tom Brady five-copy Red Flare and Caleb Williams Orange Galactic numbered to 25 remain card-specific; the Brady statement does not establish serial marking. Other colors, autograph families and the calendar release date remain unverified; guide publication dates and box contents do not establish release or pack odds.
