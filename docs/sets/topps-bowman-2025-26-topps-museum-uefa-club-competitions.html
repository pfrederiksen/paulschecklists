THE DETAILS MATTER

Official data.
Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

All 756 physical subject rows / 686 family-card identities across 35 sections retain original Name, Team and 102 literal Rookie marks. Fifteen dual and fourteen triple autograph cards and three ten-subject books retain every signer, including the page-seven/eight continuation. All 147 odds categories retain Hobby / Hat-Trick columns and nulls. The sheet names no ratio unit; Base Card 4:1 remains literal. Three guide examples stay card-specific; a 25-copy statement does not establish serial marking or an unnamed color. September 18 UK availability is regional evidence, not a verified general release. Exhaustive parallel eligibility and numbering remain unverified.
