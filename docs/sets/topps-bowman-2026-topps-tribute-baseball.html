THE DETAILS MATTER

Official data.
Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

All 786 printed subject rows across 22 sections retain their physical Name, Team and Rookie cells, including all 100 base numbers, repeated multi-subject card numbers, 15 blank broadcaster teams, literal Cleveland team cells and mixed-case TTR-Jd. Published team assignments, accents and marks are retained without correction. Rookie flags are literal source values and are not propagated between cards or subjects. All 143 categories retain literal Hobby ratios; the odds sheet does not name a ratio unit, so no per-pack probability is inferred. The July 27 official guide explicitly announces July 29 release and base Blue /150 / Black 1/1. Those limits are not applied to other families or colors. The three odds-only 2026 Tribute Career Achievement Award Continuity categories remain unjoined: they do not explicitly identify the insert or the separate Auto roster. Box contents remain separate from ratios; exhaustive parallel numbering is not confirmed.
