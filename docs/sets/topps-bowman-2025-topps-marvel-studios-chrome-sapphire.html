Official sources & import notes

THE DETAILS MATTER

Official data.
Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

All 334 numbered subject rows plus 118 unnumbered sketch artists across six sections are reconciled to tagged physical PDF cells. The 200-card Base retains separate Name, Secondary Name, Property, Phase and Debut columns; actor first/last names and Character Name remain separate on autographs. All repeated dual and trio signers, overlapping Shang-Chi property glyphs, clipped Doctor Strange property text, apparent source spellings and literal DEBUT marks are retained. All 28 odds categories preserve the complete Sapphire Pack Rate header and literal ratios. The product page mentions a 1/1 Padparadscha parallel without an exact family scope; that statement stays qualified, not propagated to every Padpradascha family. Other serial limits and the exact calendar release date remain unverified; product year 2025 is separate from the 2026 catalog listing.
