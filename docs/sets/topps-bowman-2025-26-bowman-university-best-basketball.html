Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

All 658 physical subject rows / 618 family-card identities across 17 sections are reconciled. Forty secondary signer rows retain blank printed card-number cells, including continuations across pages; GGBM Brynn McGaughy is a printed alpha-only code. Name and school cells preserve original glyph draw order, source spellings and team assignments. The PDF publishes no rookie field. All 133 Hobby odds retain literal values with no stated ratio unit. Only Let’s Go Superfractor 1/1 is joined from the guide; the AJ Dybantsa Blue 150-copy example is card-specific.
