THE DETAILS MATTER

Official data.
Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

All 730 numbered physical subjects / 710 family-card identities and 136 unnumbered artists are retained across 20 families. Original unlabelled text columns change meaning by family and are not reinterpreted as teams. Repeated 1 UP and Marquee Motion subjects remain separate. All 133 odds categories and five exact headings are preserved, including three 1:XX placeholders, the original dash plus 1:832 cell and Super Box Bonus Pack 5:1. No ratio unit is printed, so no pack rate is calculated. Only the base FoilFractor 1/1 is mapped. Guide five-copy Spider-Man and ten-copy Chrome Silver Surfer examples have no named parallel or serial marking. Calendar release and exhaustive numbering remain unverified.
