THE DETAILS MATTER

Official data.
Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

All 374 numbered Disney entries and 68 unnumbered sketch artists are reconciled, retaining actor/character/property text columns and the full sketch-artist roster, including mixed Japanese/Latin names. Paper and Chrome base tiers remain separate; apparent printed spellings, case and clipped property text are preserved. Only the product-page Platinum starting numbering is imported; pack odds and exhaustive parallel-numbering reconciliation remain pending.
