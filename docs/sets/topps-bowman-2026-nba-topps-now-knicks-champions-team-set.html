What to expect

A made-to-order 16-card bundle with 15 base cards and one additional parallel, SP, autograph or autograph-relic. The official page supplies 15 base, eight image-variation and nine autograph subject rows, plus three named redemption programs. All card numbers remain blank. Seven unjoined foil limits and three exact named 1/1 redemptions are retained; bundle PR65941 and June 13–20 sale dates are product-level facts.
