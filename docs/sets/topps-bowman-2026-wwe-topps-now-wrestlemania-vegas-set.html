What to expect

A made-to-order 15-card bundle: fourteen base cards plus one additional parallel, Legend short print, autograph or redemption. The PDF lists forty numbered entries across four programs. Five base-only foil limits are published; both autograph programs retain separate /5-or-lower qualifications. Bundle PR5847 and April 13–20 sale dates are product-level facts; WrestleMania event dates are not a general release date.
