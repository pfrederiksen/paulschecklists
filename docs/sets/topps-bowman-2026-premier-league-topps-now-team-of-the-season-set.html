What to expect

A made-to-order 12-card bundle: eleven fixed base cards plus one guaranteed chase card. All eleven published subjects and clubs are imported with blank card numbers. Six unnamed Parallel limits, two Auto limits and an explicitly unnumbered parallel remain unjoined product terms. The Description names the 2025/26 season; Specs publishes Product Year 2026 and June 11–18 sale dates. Print run, autograph roster and general release remain unverified.
