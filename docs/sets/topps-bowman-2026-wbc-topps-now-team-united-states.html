What to expect

The official Team United States WBC product publishes 12 base subjects, 12 Parallel Subjects and 4 autograph subjects. All 28 entries are imported under their original headings with blank unpublished numbers. Six product foil limits and explicitly unnumbered WBC Logo terms retain their scope. Bundle PR20322 and March 5–16 sale dates are separate from an unverified general release; historical coverage remains partial.
