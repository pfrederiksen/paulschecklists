THE DETAILS MATTER

Official data.
Clear expectations.

“1 in 24 packs” means a card from that category, across all eligible subjects. It is not the chance of pulling a particular player. Serial numbering is a print run, not a pack hit rate.

Missing values are not estimates. Original manufacturer columns and notes remain available in card details. A listed subject is not guaranteed in every parallel. Imported odds categories alone do not establish an exhaustive parallel inventory.

All 488 physical subject rows / 458 family-card identities and 17 sections are reconciled, including 268 literal Rookie marks. Original Name/Team/Rookie cells, repeated dual/booklet signers, historical team assignments, Portland Trailblazers and Persistant Marks wording remain intact. All 91 Hobby/FDI odds retain original literals and nulls with unstated ratio units. Ten exact guide specifications include nine odds joins; Holo Gold /50 remains separate from Gold Electricity.
